## A notebook with the door open

A finished idea usually hides the work that made it possible. You see the conclusion, but not the wrong turns, the questions, or the small examples that finally made it click.

A personal site can make room for that process. A short observation can become a note. A note can grow into an essay. An essay can change when a better explanation comes along.

> Writing can be a way of finding out what you think, rather than a record of something you already know.

## Give an idea something to push against

Try explaining it with one concrete example. If the explanation breaks, that is useful information. You have found the part that needs more thought.

- What exactly am I claiming?
- What would make that claim false?
- Can I explain it without relying on a familiar phrase?

The goal is not to make every piece definitive. It is to make the next version a little clearer.
